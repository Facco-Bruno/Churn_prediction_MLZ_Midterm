🧠 Business Insights and Model Justification

1️⃣ Business Understanding
The goal of this project is to predict customer churn for a telecommunications company. 
Customer churn occurs when a client stops using the service. Retaining existing customers is typically cheaper than acquiring new ones, 
so predicting churn can directly improve profitability and guide retention strategies.

2️⃣ Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
The analysis was conducted on the Telco Customer Churn dataset (IBM), which contains customer demographic information, subscription details, and billing history.

Target Distribution
- Churn rate ≈ 26%
- The dataset is slightly imbalanced, but balanced enough for standard classification models.

Numerical Features
- Tenure: Customers with short tenure are far more likely to churn — a strong negative correlation with churn.
- MonthlyCharges: Higher monthly charges are associated with increased churn probability.
- TotalCharges: Positively correlated with tenure; customers who churn early naturally accumulate lower total charges.

Categorical Features
Key categorical variables showed strong relationships with churn:
- Contract type: Customers with month-to-month contracts have a much higher churn rate compared to one- or two-year contracts.
- InternetService: Fiber optic users tend to churn more than DSL users.
- PaymentMethod: Customers paying via electronic check have significantly higher churn.
- PaperlessBilling: Slightly correlated with churn, often combined with month-to-month contracts.

Correlation Highlights
Feature | Correlation with Churn
--------------------------------
Tenure | -0.35
MonthlyCharges | +0.19
TotalCharges | -0.20

3️⃣ Model Training and Evaluation
Two models were trained and compared:
- Logistic Regression (baseline)
- Random Forest Classifier (ensemble model)

Evaluation Metric
ROC AUC was used to compare predictive power across models.

Model | ROC AUC (Validation)
-----------------------------
Logistic Regression | ~0.80
Random Forest | ~0.84

Conclusion
- The Random Forest model slightly outperformed Logistic Regression in validation ROC AUC.
- Logistic Regression remains interpretable and lightweight, making it a strong baseline.
- For deployment, either can be justified — but Random Forest was selected for best balance between accuracy and robustness.

4️⃣ Business Conclusions
- Contract length is the single most predictive factor — offering long-term contracts or incentives could drastically reduce churn.
- Service quality improvements for fiber optic customers may address one of the major churn sources.
- Payment experience matters: customers using electronic check are at higher risk — promoting autopay or credit card options may improve retention.
- Early engagement with new customers (low tenure) is critical — predictive alerts can trigger retention campaigns.

5️⃣ Model Justification
The final deployed model:
- Combines demographic, service, and billing features.
- Uses Random Forest, trained on one-hot encoded features via DictVectorizer.
- Is deployed as a REST API in Flask, fully containerized in Docker.
- Returns both:
  {"churn": true, "churn_probability": 0.65}

This setup makes it easy for downstream applications (dashboards, CRM systems, or marketing workflows) to call the model and act on real-time predictions.
